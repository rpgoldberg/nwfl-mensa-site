Northwest Florida Mensa is the local group of American Mensa for the Florida Panhandle: all of Florida west of the Apalachicola River, which is the part of the state in the Central Time Zone. We hold lunches, dinners and meetups around Fort Walton Beach, Panama City and Pensacola, and everyone is welcome to visit.

## Our history

American Mensa chartered this group as PensaMensa on 5 July 1987, when it split off from the Tallahassee local group. Its area was then the ZIP codes 32422 to 32499 and 32500 to 32599. Our first Local Secretary also chaired the group's scholarship committee for many years.

In March 2000 the group took the name Northwest Florida Mensa, to match the wider area it had covered since the last realignment of group boundaries.
