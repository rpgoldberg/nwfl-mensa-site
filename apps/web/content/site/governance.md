How the group is run: our charter and bylaws, the Executive Committee, and elections.

## Our charter

American Mensa first chartered the group as PensaMensa in 1987. Our current charter dates from 2012.

## Our bylaws

Our bylaws were adopted in 2013. Among other things, they say who may serve:

> All officers, whether elected or appointed, must be current members in good standing of AML; an officer is defined as any person who is given a title with specific responsibilities. All voting members of the ExComm must also be current members in good standing of Northwest Florida Mensa.

And what two of the offices do:

> The Membership Officer shall be responsible for contact with new and prospective members and recruitment programs.

> The Editor shall edit the newsletter. The Editor shall publish notices of meetings and programs, required ballots, results of business meetings and elections, amendments to the bylaws and related discussions and ballots, the semi-annual financial reports, and shall prepare and publish Post Office forms if required.

Copies of the charter and the bylaws will be added to this page.

## The Executive Committee

The officers form the Executive Committee (ExComm), which meets several times a year. Its approved [minutes](/minutes) are published here, newest first.

## Elections

Officers are elected in even years, and new terms begin on 1 October. Voting is by paper ballot or by email to the election chair. The 2026 election is complete.
