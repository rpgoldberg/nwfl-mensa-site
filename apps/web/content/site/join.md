Mensa is open to anyone who scores in the top 2 percent on an accepted intelligence test. You can take the Mensa admission test, or send in a qualifying score from a test you have already taken.

- [Join American Mensa](https://www.us.mensa.org/join/), where the tests and the accepted scores are listed
- [Admission for gifted youth](https://www.us.mensa.org/join/gifted-youth-admission/)

## Visit us first

Everyone is welcome at our events: lunches, dinners and meetups in the Fort Walton Beach, Panama City and Pensacola areas. See our [events](/events) for what is on, and ask any of our [officers](/officers) if you have a question.

## Your local group

American Mensa places each member in a local group by address. Members who live in the Florida Panhandle west of the Apalachicola River belong to Northwest Florida Mensa.
